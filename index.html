      The language

      What is Elixir?

      Elixir is a functional programming language for software that must scale and stay easy to maintain. It runs on the Erlang virtual machine, the BEAM, which telecom systems have used for decades to stay online.

      • It keeps running

        Each task runs in its own lightweight process. When one process fails, a supervisor restarts it and the rest of the system keeps working.

      • It scales

        The same code uses all the cores of one machine, and it can spread across a cluster of machines.

      • It is easy to change

        Immutable data and a gradual type system make code clear to read and safe to change as the team grows.

      • It is productive

        Mix, Hex, the formatter and ExUnit come with the language. With IEx and Livebook you can try code live.

      Method

      How we build this list

      We scan public LinkedIn profiles for job experiences that mention Elixir. Then we group those experiences by company.

      What counts

      • A person counts when one of their job experiences mentions Elixir: as a skill on the job, in the job title or in the job description.
      • A company is listed when 2 or more people have such an experience there.
      • In use means that at least one of those people still has the job.
      • Elixir team now counts the people who have the job today. Elixir team, all time counts everyone who had it.
      • Since and Last use are the first and the most recent year in which one of them had the job.
      • Location, sector and company size come from public company information.

      What we do not show

      We never show people, names or profile links. Team sizes show as ranges, so a listing never gives an exact number of people.

      Limits

      • We only find people who mention Elixir on a LinkedIn job experience. The profile headline and the "About" section do not count.
      • Coverage is not equal in all countries. The scan has more profiles from some regions than from others.
      • A team can still use Elixir after its "Last use" year if nobody updated their profile.

      Add your company

      1. On LinkedIn, mention Elixir on your current job: add it as a skill on the job, or write it in the job title or the job description.
      2. Ask one more person on your team to do the same. We list a company when 2 or more people use Elixir there.
      3. Send us the LinkedIn URL of the company, for example linkedin.com/company/your-company.
